Senior Manager, Data & Business Analytics · Guatemala City, working US hours

I own the analytics layer for a US direct-to-consumer beauty brand — Shopify, NetSuite, Klaviyo, GA4 and affiliate data consolidated in Snowflake, modelled with dbt, and delivered through Looker and internally hosted dashboards.

Most of what I build answers one of three questions: what should we order, why don't these two systems agree, and so what should we do about it.

What I work on
Demand forecasting — decay-curve models for the first 120 days of a launch, including a proxy-SKU method for forecasting products with no sales history at all. The output people act on is the half-life, not the coefficient.

Reconciliation at scale — record-level matching between vendor files and order data, producing an approved list and a flagged list with a reason and a dollar value on every row. Fuzzy matching with banded thresholds where there is no shared key.

Reporting that doesn't lie — scheduled refreshes behind validation gates that block publication on stale, duplicated or incomplete data, and delta-only alerting so the messages still get read six months later.

AI-assisted analytics — reusable skills for forecasting and master-data auditing, plus a written data-accuracy standard for the team. Leverage is easy; leverage without shipping a wrong number is the actual work.
